Projects

Selected doctoral projects at the intersection of plant physiology, remote sensing, and data science.
Download CV Related Publications

UAV Thermal Water Stress Mapping

Developed UAV-based thermal approaches to detect within-field water stress patterns and evaluate the influence of temporal dynamics and reference temperature selection on stress estimates.

Thermal remote sensing Water stress Spatial variability

Yield Prediction with ML + UAV

Implemented machine-learning workflows using UAV imagery to estimate wild blueberry yield, testing effects of flight altitude, sampling strategy, and model selection.

Machine learning UAV imaging Model evaluation

Leaf Spectroscopy for Turgor Loss Point Estimation

Advancing spectroscopy-based prediction of turgor loss point (TLP) to reduce reliance on destructive pressure–volume methods and accelerate screening for drought tolerance.

Plant physiology Trait prediction High-throughput phenotyping

Climate Drivers of Long-Term Productivity

Integrated climate datasets with satellite-derived productivity metrics to quantify spatiotemporal patterns in wild blueberry productivity across 134 fields over 2000–2022.

MODIS productivity Climate impacts Spatiotemporal analysis
